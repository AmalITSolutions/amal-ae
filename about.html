ABOUT US

ZAHRT AL AMAL COMPUTER TRADING L.L.C & ZAHRAT AMAL COMPUTER Software HOUSE L.L.C. are sister companies that works under the common umbrella as AMAL.

AMAL is an emerging IT solutions company in Dubai with a team of highly experienced and dynamic professionals that caters to wide range of IT requirements. The company’s core capabilities include consultancy, IT Infrastructure solution design & deployment, licensing and training services. AMAL is an experienced and trusted provider of IT solutions and professional services. We’ve been delivering expertise and all types of technology and support to business across UAE.

Our solutions span everything from software and hardware provision, network assessment and virtualization services to disaster recovery, backup, structured cabling and flexible IT support agreements, all delivered within close working partnerships with our clients and designed around their specific needs, strategic objectives and business operations. At our headquarters in Dubai, our experienced teams of consultants, engineers and support staff offer a rich source of business expertise and solid technical know-how. And we support any scale of small or medium-size business, from companies with limited or no internal IT expertise to multi-site, multinational enterprises seeking first-class IT solutions on a larger scale.

Our expertise and credibility are proven: we’re proud of our high client retention rate and accredited partnerships with best-of-breed vendors. All testify to the quality of our advice, the excellence of our consultation and service and our ability to deliver the best IT tools as they become available. Close, long-term relationships and good communication with our vendors continues to enable us to provide our customers with the features they demand, underpinned by excellent customer service and support.
