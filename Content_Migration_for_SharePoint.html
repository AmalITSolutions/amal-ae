Move and reclassify SharePoint content in minutes

Content Migrator for SharePoint is a simple, agentless and cost-effective tool that installs in minutes, and empowers site owners to move and classify SharePoint content quickly, confidently and without posing risk to the environment. Improve user experience and reduce IT burden by empowering site owners to move documents and items from legacy SharePoint, Windows file shares and Google Drive into SharePoint, Office 365 and OneDrive for Business.

  • Move content and its version history quickly and easily without data loss or downtime
  • Copy and reorganize content across SharePoint to meet business needs
  • Preserve, reassign and add metadata to content as its migrated to enhance its business value
  • Empower site owners to move and classify their own content confidently and without risk

Features


ZeroIMPACT migrations

Empower users to move and classify their own content confidently and without posing risk to the environment, using Content Migrator’s intuitive drag-and-drop or copy-and-paste interface. The simple, agentless tool installs in less than five minutes and frees IT resources to focus on more strategic projects.

Simple metadata and version history retention

Move content, metadata and version history in bulk directly into SharePoint, Office 365 and OneDrive for Business without data loss, downtime or business disruption. With Content Migrator for SharePoint, you can move documents, files and items in a flash by running multiple tasks simultaneously.

  • Move legacy SharePoint documents, list items and folders to new versions of SharePoint on-premises and online
  • Upload files and folders from Windows file shares or any file hosting service that synchronizes locally, such as Dropbox and Box
  • Migrate shared and personal documents, as well as entire folders, from Google Drive to Office 365 and OneDrive for Business quickly and easily

Rapid reorganization

Copy, move and reorganize SharePoint documents, list items and folders between sites, site collections and server farms to simplify information access, optimize storage and meet the evolving needs of your site owners and the business.

Meta value

Retain, reassign and add essential document metadata such as created and modified timestamps and authors while migrating and reorganizing content to and within SharePoint. This makes information easier to find and enhances its business value.
